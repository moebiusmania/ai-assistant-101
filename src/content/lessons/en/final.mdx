## The course in 8 sentences

1. An AI assistant **writes the most likely text**, one word at a time: that’s why it’s good with words but can **make things up confidently**.
2. It has a **knowledge cutoff** and **limited memory**: open new chats and repeat key information.
3. **AI Fluency** = working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely.
4. **Delegation**: know what you want, know the tool, split the work. The higher the risk, the more you check.
5. **Description**: context, goal, constraints, format, tone. And ask the AI to ask you questions.
6. **Discernment**: evaluate product, process and performance. Watch out for numbers, quotes and links.
7. **Diligence**: no sensitive data, be transparent, and remember **if you sign it, it’s yours**.
8. Working with AI is a **loop**: ask, evaluate, adjust, repeat.
