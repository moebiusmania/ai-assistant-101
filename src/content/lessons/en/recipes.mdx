Now that you know the 4Ds, here’s a collection of **ready-made requests** for everyday life. They work with any assistant. The parts <mark class="ph">[in square brackets]</mark> are for you to replace with your own information.

<Callout type="tip">
  <p>These templates are a starting point, not a magic formula. After the first answer, <strong>keep the conversation going</strong>: “shorter”, “simpler”, “give me an alternative”.</p>
</Callout>

## Writing and communicating

<div class="recipes">
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Tricky email"
    icon="pen"
    when="Cancellations, complaints, delicate requests"
    prompt={'I need to write an email to [recipient] about [reason].\nHere’s the situation: [explain in 2-3 sentences].\nI’d like a [polite but firm / friendly / formal] tone.\n[Number] lines max. Use [NAME] instead of personal details.'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Fix my text"
    icon="wand"
    when="Correcting without rewriting"
    prompt={'Fix grammar and punctuation mistakes in this text, without changing its style.\nThen list the main changes you made.\n\nText: [paste here]'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Change the tone"
    icon="chat"
    when="When a message sounds too harsh (or too soft)"
    prompt={'Rewrite this message to be [kinder / firmer / shorter], keeping the same meaning. Give me 2 versions.\n\nMessage: [paste here]'}
  />
</div>

## Understanding and learning

<div class="recipes">
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Explain it simply"
    icon="lightbulb"
    when="Hard concepts, articles, technical terms"
    prompt={'Explain [topic] as if I were 12.\nUse an everyday example.\nAt the end, ask me 2 questions to check I understood.'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Personal tutor"
    icon="brain"
    when="Studying a subject or a language"
    prompt={'I want to learn [topic]. My level is [beginner / intermediate].\nBe a patient teacher: explain one concept at a time, then give me an exercise and correct me.\nDon’t give me the solution straight away: give me a hint if I get it wrong.'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Decode a document"
    icon="search"
    when="Bills, contracts, official letters (without personal data!)"
    prompt={'I’m pasting a [bill / contract / letter]. I’ve removed the personal data.\nExplain in plain words:\n1. What it’s about\n2. What I need to do and by when\n3. Anything to watch out for\n\nDocument: [paste here]'}
  />
</div>

<Callout type="warn">
  <p>For important legal, medical or financial documents, use AI to <strong>understand and prepare your questions</strong>, then check with a professional.</p>
</Callout>

## Organising and deciding

<div class="recipes">
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Useful summary"
    icon="layers"
    when="Articles, minutes, long documents"
    prompt={'Summarise this text in [5] key points.\nThen write in one sentence the most important thing to remember.\nIf there are deadlines or actions, list them separately.\n\nText: [paste here]'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Plan a trip"
    icon="globe"
    when="Day trips, holidays, weekends"
    prompt={'Plan [number] days in [place] in [month].\nWe are [who: a couple, a family with kids aged X…], we like [interests], we don’t like [things to avoid].\nRough budget: [amount].\nGive me a day-by-day plan. Tell me what’s worth booking in advance.'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Pros and cons"
    icon="compass"
    when="Choices and comparisons"
    prompt={'I need to choose between [option A] and [option B].\nWhat matters to me: [criteria].\nMake a table of pros and cons for each, then tell me which questions I should ask myself to decide.\nDon’t decide for me.'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Weekly menu"
    icon="sparkle"
    when="Shopping and cooking"
    prompt={'Plan [5] dinners for [number] people.\nConstraints: [vegetarian / gluten-free / budget / little time].\nThen make a shopping list grouped by aisle.'}
  />
</div>

## Practising and preparing

<div class="recipes">
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Mock interview"
    icon="chat"
    when="Job interviews, oral exams"
    prompt={'Run a mock interview for the role of [role].\nAsk me one question at a time and wait for my answer.\nAfter each answer give me honest feedback: what works and what to improve.'}
  />
  <CopyPrompt
    title="Honest critic"
    icon="eye"
    when="When you want a real opinion, not compliments"
    prompt={'Here is [my idea / my text / my project]: [paste here].\nPlay devil’s advocate: list the 3 weakest points and what could go wrong.\nBe direct, no need to be nice.'}
  />
</div>
