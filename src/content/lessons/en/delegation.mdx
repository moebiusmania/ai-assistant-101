The first question isn’t *“how do I ask?”*, but **“does it make sense to ask AI?”**. Delegating well means three things.

## 1. Know what you want (before opening the chat)

It sounds obvious, but it’s the most common mistake: people open the assistant “to see what happens”. Stop for 20 seconds and ask yourself:

- **What’s the goal?** Not “write an email”, but “get a refund from the shop”.
- **What matters to me?** Speed? Accuracy? A personal touch?
- **How will I know the result is good?**

<Callout type="tip" title="The 20-second trick">
  <p>Try saying out loud what you want to achieve, in one sentence. If you can’t, the AI won’t be able to guess it for you.</p>
</Callout>

## 2. Know your tool

Every assistant has strengths and limits. Before handing something over, ask yourself:

- Can it **search the web**, or does it only work with what it learned?
- Can it **read files**, photos, PDFs?
- Where does the data I give it end up? (More on this in the Diligence lesson.)

If you don’t know, ask the assistant itself. But remember: what it says about itself can be wrong too. For important things, check the settings or the service’s help page.

## 3. Split the work

The choice is rarely “all to AI” or “all to me”. Often the best solution is to **share**:

| Usually fine for AI | Usually better done by you |
|---|---|
| First draft of a text | Important decisions (health, money, law) |
| Summarising, reordering, rephrasing | Anything that needs your memories and feelings |
| Finding ideas and alternatives | Checking facts and figures |
| Explaining concepts simply | Very personal messages (condolences, real apologies) |
| Being a “gym”: simulations, quizzes | Judging people (hiring, assessments) |

## How much does a mistake “cost”?

A simple rule for deciding: **the higher the cost of a mistake, the more you must check** (or do it yourself).

<div class="risk">

- 🟢 **Low** — gift ideas, a recipe, a playlist name. If it’s wrong, no big deal.
- 🟡 **Medium** — a work email, a summary for studying. Reread carefully.
- 🔴 **High** — health, money, legal documents, safety. Use AI only to understand and prepare, then check with a professional.

</div>
